whoami

I'm an engineer who's happiest a few layers below the UI — designing the API contracts, the data model, and the queues that keep a system honest under load. Most of my production work is node, nestjs, go, and spring boot, glued to Postgres, Mongo, Redis, and Kafka.

The thing I keep coming back to is developer tooling. My side projects are mostly CLIs and local-first tools — the kind of software you live inside a terminal with. I learn by building them, reading the source of things I depend on, and getting out of the house to talk to other people who do the same.
